# What's in nekozame os

## Desktop

- niri (window manager)
- DankMaterialShell (shell: bar, launcher, notifications, settings)
- dms-greeter (login screen)
- cava (audio visualizer on the bar)
- Ghostty (terminal)
- fuzzel (launcher)
- Thunar (file manager)
- yazi (terminal file manager)
- Doom Emacs (editor, `$EDITOR`)
- Zen (browser)
- Helium (browser, optional)
- gum (the setup's menus)

On the Steam Deck image, Desktop Mode is KDE Plasma and niri is optional.

## Browser

- Dark Reader (dark mode on every site)
- uBlock Origin (ad and tracker blocker)
- Privacy Badger (tracker blocker)
- LibRedirect (privacy frontends for YouTube, Reddit and more)
- ClearURLs (strips tracking from links)
- Consent-O-Matic (rejects cookie banners)
- DuckDuckGo (search)
- HTTPS-only, strict tracking protection, no telemetry, no WebRTC leaks

## Audio

- low-latency PipeWire with realtime priority
- qpwgraph (patchbay)
- Wine (Windows apps)

## Music production (optional)

- Bitwig, Reaper, Renoise, Ardour or LMMS (DAWs)
- Carla (plugin host)
- Calf, LSP, Surge XT, Dexed, Cardinal, Helm, Geonkick, x42, Zam, Dragonfly Reverb (plugins)
- yabridge (Windows plugins)

## Streaming and VMs

- Tailscale (private network between your machines)
- Sunshine (game streaming, always on over Tailscale)
- quickemu (Windows VMs)
- virt-manager (VMs)

## Look

- nekozame theme (DMS, Ghostty, Emacs, KDE, Konsole, btop, Dark Reader)
- animated catshark boot screen
- animated catshark wallpaper

## System

- greenboot (rolls back an update that doesn't reach the login screen)
- signed daily builds, `:stable` weekly

## Removed

- KDE Plasma and the KDE apps (PC images)
